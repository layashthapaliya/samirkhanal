HKUST Team

Current HKUST Members

Research Assistant Professor, Postdoctoral researchers, Ph.D. and MPhil students advancing environmental biotechnology research at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology.

Research Assistant Professor
Dr. Surendra K.C.
Research Assistant Professor at HKUST focused on bioconversion of organic resources via anaerobic digestion, nanobubble technology, aquaponics/bioponics, and macroalgae-based biorefinery. Editor of Bioresource Technology Reports. Listed as Top 2% Scientist by Elsevier and Stanford University.
Postdoctoral Researcher
Dr. Mei Zhou
Joined HKUST in August 2025 with Ph.D. from University of Tübingen, Germany. Focuses on developing microaeration strategies to enhance anaerobic digestion for waste(water) treatment and resource recovery.
Postdoctoral Researcher
Dr. Shuo Wang
Joined HKUST in August 2025 with Ph.D. from Shandong University, China. Focuses on nanobubble technology to enhance phytoremediation for improving plant-based removal of environmental pollutants.
Ph.D. Student
Jie Meng
Specializes in sustainable waste treatment and resource recovery. Integrates experimental studies of anaerobic digestion with machine learning modeling to advance biowaste valorization.
MPhil Student
Gokarna Chand
Gokarna Chand is a MPhil student in our group. He holds a B.E. in Chemical Engineering from Tribhuvan University, Nepal. His research includes pyrolysis, biomass to energy conversion, ligno-cellulosic biomass extraction, and anaerobic digestion.
MPhil Student
Nicolas Paim Patricio
Nicolas Patricio is a MPhil student in our group. He holds a B.S. in Agricultural Engineering from University of São Paulo, Brazil. His research focuses on nanobubbles in hydroponic systems, vertical farming, and high-value crops like strawberries and cherry tomatoes.
MPhil Student
Layas Thapaliya
Layas Thapaliya is a MPhil student in our group at HKUST. He holds a B.E in Chemical Engineering from Tribhuvan University, Nepal. His research focuses on environmental biotechnology, including biological treatment processes for sustainable resource recovery and waste valorization.
